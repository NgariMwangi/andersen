How this works

  • Applies to {{ active_count }} active employee(s).
  • Uses leave type ANNUAL{% if annual %} ({{ annual.name }}){% endif %}.
  • Creates leave with status Booked immediately and updates annual leave balances.
  • If an employee already has approved or booked leave on some of those days, only the remaining days are booked — no double-count.
  • Days before an employee’s hire date are not charged.
  • Employees who join later get these periods automatically when created/reactivated as active, or via Sync to new joiners.
  • Employees already fully covered still appear away via their existing leave.

Day counting follows the Annual leave type rule (working or calendar days) and excludes public holidays.
